### 🔍 Case Study: LLM Output Evaluation & QA for Interactive Web Components

**Brief Description:**
Analyzed and stress-tested the performance of advanced generative AI models (Large Language Models) in producing interactive web interface components (HTML/Tailwind CSS) based on functionality, logical accuracy, and instruction adherence.

**Tasks & Responsibilities:**
- **Functional Testing:** Evaluated AI-generated interactive cost calculator widgets to ensure all UI features (e.g., real-time calculations and export/print buttons) functioned according to specifications.
- **Defect Tagging:** Identified and documented discrepancies between AI text descriptions and actual interface behavior (e.g., failed visual states of checkboxes and inactive print buttons).
- **Multi-Dimensional Assessment:** Provided structured evaluations across 7 key dimensions (including Outcome Quality, Trust & Grounding, and Collaboration Quality) supported by comprehensive rationales in English.
- **AI Consistency Tool Utilization:** Leveraged internal evaluation calibration tools (Compass) to ensure scoring consistency prior to final qualification.


### 📊 Case Study: Global AI Data Annotation & Model Training

**Brief Description:**
Contributed as a data evaluation specialist for a large-scale global Large Language Model (LLM) training project (via DataAnnotation). Focused on improving the quality, accuracy, and safety of AI responses through Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) assessments.

**Tasks & Responsibilities:**
- **Multilingual Evaluation:** Leveraged linguistic expertise in Indonesian, Sundanese, and English to assess cultural nuances, grammar, and local context in AI-generated responses.
- **AI Response Evaluation:** Analyzed and benchmarked outputs from various generative AI models based on factuality, tone, and strict adherence to user instructions.
- **Bias & Hallucination Detection:** Identified and flagged inaccuracies, logical flaws, and hallucinations in AI-generated text to mitigate misinformation risks.
- **Complex Guideline Adherence:** Navigated and applied extensive, continuously updated rubric guidelines to ensure consistent, high-quality training data sets for machine learning models.

**Brief Description:**

### 🎙️ Case Study: Text-to-Speech (TTS) & Audio Naturalness Evaluation

**Brief Description:**
Evaluated and validated the output quality of large-scale Text-to-Speech (TTS) AI models. Focused on rigorous audio testing to ensure the AI generated voices with high naturalness, proper intonation, and human-like conversational immersion.

**Tasks & Responsibilities:**
- **Audio Naturalness Assessment:** Critically listened to and rated AI-synthesized speech based on clarity, prosody (rhythm), pacing, and emotional tone to match the provided text context.
- **Audio Artifact Detection:** Identified, flagged, and documented audio anomalies such as robotic glitches, unnatural breathing sounds, mechanical distortion, or phonetic mispronunciations.
- **Multilingual Immersion Testing:** Verified accent accuracy and dialect nuances across multiple languages (including Indonesian, Sundanese, and English) to ensure the AI voice maintained strict conversational immersion without sounding rigid.
- **Model Benchmarking (A/B Testing):** Conducted comparative evaluations of multiple TTS audio samples to determine which model provided the most seamless speech-to-speech communication experience.
